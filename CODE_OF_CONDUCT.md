# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Expected Behavior

✅ **Do:**
- Be respectful and kind
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully
- Focus on what's best for the community
- Use inclusive language

❌ **Don't:**
- Use inappropriate language or imagery
- Engage in trolling, harassment, or personal attacks
- Share private information without permission
- Disrupt community discussions or events

## Enforcement

**Reporting:** Contact project maintainers via GitHub issues or email [INSERT EMAIL ADDRESS]

**Consequences:** Violations may result in warnings, temporary bans, or permanent removal from the community.

**Privacy:** All reports are handled confidentially.

*This Code of Conduct applies to all community spaces and when representing the project publicly.*
